Eliezer

A private personal assistant.

Eliezer helps one person — its owner — stay on top of everyday life: it reads the owner's email and calendars, keeps notes on plans and open tasks, prepares draft replies for the owner to review, and sends the owner a short daily summary.

Eliezer is not a public product. It isn't offered to anyone else, and no one else can sign in to it.

Read the privacy policy for exactly what Eliezer accesses and how that data is handled.
